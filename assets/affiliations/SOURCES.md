# Institutional mark sources

The marks in this directory identify the authors' affiliations. They are not project logos and do not imply institutional endorsement. They are excluded from any GenCoord project license and remain governed by their respective institutions.

All assets were retrieved on 2026-08-23 and kept at their official proportions.

| File | Institution | Official source | Processing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `tsinghua.png` | Tsinghua University | [Tsinghua visual identity: standard combinations](https://vi.tsinghua.edu.cn/gk/xxbz/bzzh1.htm) | External whitespace trimmed; mark geometry and color unchanged. |
| `bnbu.svg` | Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University | [BNBU official homepage](https://www.bnbu.edu.cn/) and [visual identity downloads](https://mpro.bnbu.edu.cn/info/1414/4093.htm) | Official inline homepage SVG extracted without geometric or color edits. |
| `unc_charlotte.png` | University of North Carolina at Charlotte | [UNC Charlotte logo system](https://brand.charlotte.edu/visual-identity/logo-system) | Official horizontal collaboration mark; unchanged. |
| `uc_san_diego.png` | University of California San Diego | [UC San Diego brand downloads](https://brand.ucsd.edu/using-the-brand/downloads/index.html) | Official Blue/Gold digital PNG from the campus logo kit; unchanged. |

When replacing or resizing these files, follow each institution's clear-space, minimum-size, color, and non-distortion requirements.
